Do Ants Have Rulers?

Argument

The Bible says ants don't have a ruler, but they do have a ruler. Their ruler is the queen ant.

Passage

Proverbs 6:6-8

Short Answer

The queen ant just lays eggs, and gives no instructions to any other ants. [1]
